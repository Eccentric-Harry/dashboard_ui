# Camp sound bank — credits

The effects `features/goals/camp-sound.ts` plays. Every file was changed from its source:
mixed to mono, DC offset removed, leading silence trimmed, tail faded, loudness levelled,
resampled to 32 kHz 16-bit WAV.

## Sources

**Material Design sound resources** by Google, licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Original set: <https://archive.org/details/material-design-sound-resources>.
The adapted files below are shared under the same licence.

**Kenney** audio packs (Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Casino Audio, Music
Jingles) by Kenney Vleugels, <https://kenney.nl>, dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
